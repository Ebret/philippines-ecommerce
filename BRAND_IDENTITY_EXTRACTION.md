## 🔤 B. Typography (Font System)

### Font Families
- **Primary Font (Sans-serif):** System fonts
  - Stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
  - Used for: Headings, body text, UI elements
  - Style: Modern, clean, professional
  - Weight: 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold)

- **Mono Font:** Fira Code / Courier New
  - Stack: `"Fira Code", "Courier New", monospace`
  - Used for: Code, technical content (minimal use)

### Typography Hierarchy

**Headings:**
- H1: 48px (3rem) - Bold, gradient text
- H2: 36px (2.25rem) - Bold, gradient text
- H3: 24px (1.5rem) - Bold, solid color
- H4: 20px (1.25rem) - Semibold

**Body Text:**
- Large: 18px (1.125rem) - Regular
- Regular: 16px (1rem) - Regular
- Small: 14px (0.875rem) - Regular
- Extra Small: 12px (0.75rem) - Regular

**Font Weights:**
- Regular: 400
- Semibold: 600
- Bold: 700

**Line Height:**
- Headings: 1.2
- Body: 1.5-1.6
- Compact: 1.25

## 📸 D. Product Photography Style

### Photography Style
- **Type:** Lifestyle + Minimalist
- **Approach:** Clean, modern, health-focused
- **Emoji-based:** Uses emoji icons (🍃, 💊, 🌿) for product representation
- **Gradient backgrounds:** Soft gradient overlays for visual interest

### Color Grading
- **Light Mode:** Bright, clean, high contrast
- **Dark Mode:** Soft, warm, reduced contrast
- **Filters:** Subtle gradients, no heavy filters
- **Saturation:** Natural, not oversaturated

### Composition
- **Layout:** Centered, symmetrical
- **Spacing:** Generous white space
- **Cards:** Rounded corners (12-16px), subtle shadows
- **Hover Effects:** Scale up (105%), enhanced shadows

### Visual Patterns
- **Product Cards:** 3-column grid on desktop, 1-column on mobile
- **Badges:** "Popular", "Best Seller", "Premium" labels
- **Ratings:** 5-star system with amber color
- **Icons:** Lucide React icons (modern, minimal)

## 🗣️ B. Tone of Voice & Brand Messaging

### Brand Personality
- **Tone:** Friendly, professional, health-conscious
- **Voice:** Approachable, trustworthy, knowledgeable
- **Style:** Conversational yet authoritative

### Key Brand Values
1. **100% Natural** - Pure herbal ingredients
2. **Organic Certified** - Trusted quality standards
3. **Fast Results** - Proven effectiveness
4. **Wellness Journey** - Customer-focused health transformation

### Target Audience Tone
- **Primary:** Health-conscious adults (25-55 years)
- **Secondary:** Wellness enthusiasts, natural product advocates
- **Tone for them:** Supportive, educational, empowering
- **Language:** Simple, clear, benefit-focused

### Brand Messaging Examples
- "Premium Herbal Wellness Solutions"
- "Discover nature's most powerful herbal products"
- "Join thousands of satisfied customers"
- "Transform your health with our products"

### Key Phrases
- "Premium herbal wellness products"
- "100% Natural"
- "Organic Certified"
- "Fast Results"
- "Proven effectiveness"
- "Health food shop"
- "Trusted by thousands"
