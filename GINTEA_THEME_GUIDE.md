## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The GinTea-inspired theme captures the essence of tranquility and wellness through:
- **Soft, natural color palette** - Matcha greens, herbal browns, honey golds
- **Warm, inviting tones** - Like stepping into a cozy tea room
- **High contrast for readability** - 9:1 body text, 8.5:1 headings
- **Botanical authenticity** - Colors inspired by real tea varieties
- **Calming aesthetic** - Promotes relaxation and natural healing

## 🎯 Design Principles

### **1. Tranquility & Wellness**
- Soft, calming colors that promote relaxation
- Warm tones that create a welcoming atmosphere
- Natural palette inspired by real tea and herbs

### **2. Authenticity**
- Colors derived from actual tea varieties
- Matcha green, herbal brown, honey gold
- Botanical accents: mint, chamomile, sage, cinnamon

### **3. High Contrast**
- All text exceeds WCAG AA standards
- Dark mode maintains excellent readability
- Clear visual hierarchy

### **4. Natural Materials**
- Cream background like aged tea paper
- Brown tones like wood and bark
- Green tones like fresh tea leaves

### **5. Warm & Inviting**
- Honey gold accents add warmth
- Herbal browns create coziness
- Soft cream backgrounds feel natural

## 🛠️ Usage Guidelines

### **Primary Color (Matcha Green)**
- **Use for:** Main CTAs, active states, brand elements
- **Pairs with:** Cream background, honey gold accent
- **Avoid:** Overuse - reserve for important actions

### **Secondary Color (Herbal Brown)**
- **Use for:** Navigation, footer, product categories
- **Pairs with:** Matcha green, cream background
- **Avoid:** Large text blocks (use foreground instead)

### **Accent Color (Honey Gold)**
- **Use for:** Special offers, featured products, highlights
- **Pairs with:** Deep green text, matcha green
- **Avoid:** Backgrounds (use as accents only)

### **Botanical Colors**
- **Use for:** Product badges, category tags, decorative elements
- **Pairs with:** Cream background, white cards
- **Avoid:** Primary text (use for accents only)

## 📝 Typography Recommendations

### **Font Pairing**
- **Headings:** Libre Baskerville (serif) - Traditional, elegant
- **Body:** DM Sans (sans-serif) - Clean, modern, readable
- **Accent:** Honey gold or matcha green for emphasis

### **Line Heights**
- **Body:** 1.7 (comfortable reading)
- **Headings:** 1.1-1.35 (tighter, more impactful)
