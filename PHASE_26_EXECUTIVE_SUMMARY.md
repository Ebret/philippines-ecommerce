## 🎯 OVERVIEW

Phase 26 implements comprehensive enhancements to the Philippines E-Commerce Platform's product management system, focusing on:

1. **Inventory Management** (7 features) - Core operational functionality
2. **Discount & Promotion** (6 features) - Revenue generation
3. **Product Image Management** (6 features) - User experience
4. **Additional Product Features** (8 features) - Operational efficiency

---

## 📊 CURRENT STATE vs. DESIRED STATE

### Inventory Management
**Current:** Basic inventory tracking with InventoryItem and InventoryMovement models  
**Desired:** Real-time tracking, adjustments, history, multi-location, forecasting, scanning, import/export

### Discount & Promotion
**Current:** GroupDeal, BulkDiscount, FlashSale schemas (backend only)  
**Desired:** Complete UI, coupon system, campaign scheduler, rules engine, analytics

### Product Images
**Current:** Basic image upload and sorting  
**Desired:** Bulk upload, optimization, gallery, cropping, variants, CDN integration

### Additional Features
**Current:** Basic product CRUD  
**Desired:** Variant management, category/tag management, review moderation, comparison, import/export, duplication, search enhancements, analytics
